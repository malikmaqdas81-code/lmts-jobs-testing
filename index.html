The Gold Standard STAR Framework

Structure your behavioral answers with this proven 4-stage formula used by FAANG, Fortune 500, and top IT service recruiters:

S - Situation
15% of your answer

Set the context, project, company, timeline, and obstacles faced.

T - Task
15% of your answer

Clarify your personal responsibility, challenge, or sprint goal.

A - Action
55% of your answer

The core! Steps YOU took, leadership demonstrated, and technologies chosen.

R - Result
15% of your answer

Quantified outcome (e.g. 40% faster, 99.9% uptime, zero regressions).
